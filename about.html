Who we are

A structural engineering consultancy for the places people watch, play and train.

Willis Hazell Engineers was set up in 2023 by Martin Willis and Rob Hazell. Between them they bring over 50 years’ experience and significant involvement in more than 100 sports and leisure projects.

Founders

Martin Willis

Co-founder · Chartered Structural Engineer

Formerly Director and Head of Sport at Ramboll. Martin has a record of delivering infrastructure projects in sport, entertainment and leisure, many of national and international scale.

He starts by understanding what clients and stakeholders need, tailors the engineering to it, and brings disciplines together so the design is coordinated and delivers value.

Martin@willishazell.com

Rob Hazell

Co-founder · Chartered Structural Engineer

Rob has over 25 years’ contractor and consultancy experience, mainly in sport and leisure. He is known for practical, buildable and cost-effective engineering.

He advises on feasibility, planning, concept design, construction techniques, procurement and risk, and currently leads the practice’s work on sustainability and net zero carbon strategies.

Rob@willishazell.com

How we add value

Four principles shape every scheme we design.

  1. We challenge

    We question conventional approaches and test assumptions with architects, clients and designers, looking for lower cost, lower embodied carbon, shorter programmes, reduced risk and added value.

  2. We design to meet your budget

    We work to understand the financial drivers, build realistic cost plans and test our designs with the supply chain.

  3. We produce designs to be built

    Buildability is central from the start: simple, efficient construction sequences, validated with contractors.

  4. We design for low carbon

    We track embodied carbon on all our designs to deliver the lowest feasible carbon outcome.
