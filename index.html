The practice

Willis Hazell Engineers was set up in 2023 by Martin Willis and Rob Hazell, both Chartered Structural Engineers. We provide high-value design solutions for stadia, arenas, training grounds and leisure venues, and we are collaborative, courteous and quick to make clear decisions.
